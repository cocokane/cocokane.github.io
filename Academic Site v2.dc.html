I.Research Interests

Electronic structure

Using and developing First-Principles methods (primarily DFT) to understand exactly how materials work.

Novel Interconnects

Exploring novel materials for their feasibility as interconnects - the wires that connect transistors in our chips. 

ML for materials

Developing Machine-learned interatomic potentials and screening tools for high-throughput materials discovery.

III.Projects

ML-driven optimization of Lennard-Jones parameters for MD simulations

Gaussian-process regression with genetic algorithms to fit force-field parameters, improving transport-property accuracy in Li-ion battery simulations.

Crack prediction in continuous steel casting

Industrial project with ArcelorMittal–Nippon Steel: random-forest feature analysis and RNNs on plant sensor data to identify crack-inducing casting parameters.

Sports biomechanics with computer vision

Custom YOLO detection and 3D pose pipelines (AlphaPose, MotionBERT) quantifying javelin-throw biomechanics from tournament video.
