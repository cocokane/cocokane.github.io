I am a PhD student in Materials Science & Engineering at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI), working with [Prof. Ravishankar Sundararaman](https://faculty.rpi.edu/ravishankar-sundararaman) (joined Fall 2025). My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and computational materials science, with a focus on **semiconductor interconnects** and **computational materials science** — developing data-driven methods to understand and design materials at the atomic scale.

My broader interests span machine-learned interatomic potentials (MLIPs) for complex alloy systems, force-field parameterization using active learning, and molecular dynamics simulations. I am passionate about building efficient computational workflows that bridge atomistic simulations with modern ML techniques to tackle problems in next-generation semiconductor materials and beyond.

Before RPI, I completed a Dual Major B.Tech in Materials Science Engineering and Computer Science Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar (2020–2025), where I worked on LJ force-field optimization using Gaussian Process Regression and Genetic Algorithms (published in JCTC), and benchmarked machine-learned potentials for the CoCrFeMnNi Cantor alloy. I also interned at the University of Washington (ML screening of vitrimers) and TCS Research (MPC for acoustic wave separators).
